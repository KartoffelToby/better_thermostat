A small, well-insulated bedroom with a direct-valve Sonoff TRVZB needs very different settings than a drafty old-building living room or an underfloor heating zone. Answer six quick questions below to get a tailored **Screen 1 & Screen 2** starting profile.

<BtConfigurator client:load></BtConfigurator>

## How to apply these settings in Home Assistant

1. Open **Settings → Devices & Services → Better Thermostat**.
2. Click **Configure** next to the room (or **+ Add Integration → Better Thermostat** for a new room).
3. On **Screen 1**, enter the recommended **`Tolerance`**, **window/door open & close delays**, and **outdoor shutdown threshold**.
4. Click **Submit** to open **Screen 2**, then pick the recommended **`Calibration type`**, **`Calibration mode`** (plus **`MPC v2 room size`** if using MPC v2), and **`Overheating protection?`**.

Want to see the exact numbers behind how each option works? Read **[Calibration types & algorithms](/calibration_algorithms/)** and **[All settings reference](/configuration/)**.
