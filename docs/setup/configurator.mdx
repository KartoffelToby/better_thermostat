A well-insulated bedroom with a direct-valve **Sonoff TRVZB** or **Shelly BLU TRV** needs completely different settings than a covered radiator behind curtains, an **868 MHz HomematicIP** valve, or an underfloor heating zone.

Answer **six quick questions** below about your valve hardware, radiator layout, room sensor, and optional **Better Thermostat 2.0** features (separate door contacts, AC cooling, presets, or fireplace/boiler learning pauses). You will get exact **Screen 1** and **Screen 2** values matching Home Assistant's setup dialog—and you can click any summary tag afterward to compare "what-if" changes live.

## How to apply your profile in Home Assistant

1. Open **Settings → Devices & Services → Better Thermostat**.
2. Click **Configure** next to an existing room (or **+ Add Integration → Better Thermostat** for a new room).
3. **Screen 1 (Room, Sensors & Presets)**: Enter the recommended **`Tolerance`** and **outdoor shutdown threshold** in your Home Assistant system temperature unit (`0.2` / `0.3` / `0.5 °C` corresponds to `~0.4` / `~0.5` / `~0.9 °F`; `15–17 °C` corresponds to `~59–63 °F`), along with the **`hh:mm:ss` window and door delays**, **optional cooling device**, and **`Enabled Presets`**, then click **Submit**.
4. **Screen 2 (Per-TRV Hardware & Control Settings)**: Pick the recommended **`Calibration type`** and **`Calibration mode`** (plus **`MPC v2 room size`** when using MPC v2) and check the hardware toggles. *(If you grouped 2+ radiators in one room, Screen 2 repeats once for each TRV.)*
5. **Device Page (Post-Setup)**: Open the newly created Better Thermostat device in Home Assistant to set your preset temperatures (`Eco`, `Comfort`, `Sleep`, `Away`), cap oversized radiators via **`Valve Max Opening`**, or automate **`Pause Learning`**.

Want to see the exact math and benchmark numbers behind each option? Read **[Calibration types & algorithms](/calibration_algorithms/)**, **[Supported devices & compatibility](/working-devices/compatibility/)**, and **[All settings reference](/configuration/)**.
